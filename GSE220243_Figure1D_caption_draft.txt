Figure 1D. Single-cell localization of MMP13 in human cartilage. Left, RPCA-integrated UMAP showing broad transcriptional states assigned from cluster markers and established cartilage signatures. Right, localization of MMP13-positive singlets on the same embedding. MMP13 detection was sparse and observed across several states, with the strongest signal in fibrocartilage and hypertrophic chondrocyte states. RegC, regulatory chondrocytes; EC, effector chondrocytes; RepC, reparative chondrocytes; PreFC, pre-fibrocartilage chondrocytes; FC, fibrocartilage chondrocytes; MTC-like, metallothionein/stress chondrocytes; HomC, homeostatic chondrocytes; HTC, hypertrophic chondrocytes.
