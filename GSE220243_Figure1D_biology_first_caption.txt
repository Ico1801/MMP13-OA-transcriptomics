Figure 1D. Single-cell localization of MMP13 in human cartilage. Left, RPCA-integrated UMAP showing broad cartilage transcriptional states assigned from cluster markers and established cartilage signatures. Right, MMP13-positive singlets displayed on the same embedding and colored by their annotated state. MMP13 detection was sparse and distributed across several chondrocyte states, with the largest number of positive cells observed in the fibrocartilage state and additional detections in regulatory, reparative, pre-fibrocartilage, effector, and hypertrophic states. RegC, regulatory chondrocytes; EC, effector chondrocytes; RepC, reparative chondrocytes; PreFC, pre-fibrocartilage chondrocytes; FC, fibrocartilage chondrocytes; MTC-like, metallothionein/stress chondrocytes; HomC, homeostatic chondrocytes; HTC, hypertrophic chondrocytes.
